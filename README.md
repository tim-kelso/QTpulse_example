# QT Pulse: School Wellbeing Report

This report provides school leaders with insights on their school environment, including teaching and learning culture, morale, collaboration, and trust.

We developed this report and supporting data pipeline to reduce the burden on school leaders to survey their own staff.
We provide the survey hosted on our survey platform, then process and present the data.
Schools need only to dedicate 15-20 mins in a meeting for staff to complete the survey.
After that, the PDF report is delivered via email (and formatted for colour or B+W printing).
